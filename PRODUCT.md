# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring/recruiting readers and developer peers visiting a personal portfolio. They should quickly understand Himanshu Shekhar's engineering credibility, automation background, web-stack fluency, AI-tooling curiosity, and how to contact him. The context is brief, high-signal scanning rather than deep product use.

## Product Purpose

Present Himanshu as a playful, technically sharp software engineer through a terminal-native personal site. Success means a visitor can grasp who he is, what he works on, what he is exploring now, and where to reach him without losing the distinctive CLI identity.

## Brand Personality

Playful, technical, sharp. The site should feel like a competent engineer's terminal session with a mischievous streak: memorable and hackerish, but still readable, credible, and intentional.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS portfolio: gradient cards, hero-metric templates, startup sameness, and template polish without personality.
- Corporate résumé page: sterile recruiter-first layouts that erase the terminal voice.
- Unreadable terminal cosplay: tiny text, low contrast, hidden links, motion gimmicks, or jokes that block scanning.

## Design Principles

- Terminal identity is the frame, not an excuse: every interaction should preserve the CLI voice while staying legible and navigable.
- Credibility before gimmicks: playful details should reinforce engineering taste, not compete with the core introduction and contact path.
- Fast scan, deeper reward: first-time visitors should understand the page in seconds; developer peers can discover the sharper jokes and terminal affordances after.
- Be specific, not template-polished: copy and layout should sound like Himanshu, not a generic portfolio generator.
- Motion must respect control: typewriter and cursor behavior should add personality without hiding content or violating reduced-motion needs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Preserve sufficient text contrast, semantic structure, keyboard/focus support, and visible links. Provide reduced-motion alternatives for typewriter/cursor effects so the terminal personality does not make the page harder to use.
